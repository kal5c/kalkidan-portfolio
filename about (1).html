MY JOURNEY

From Computer Science to Full-Stack Development

I graduated with a degree in Computer Science from Admas University. My academic background gave me a strong foundation in programming, problem solving, databases, and computer systems.

As I continued developing my skills, I became particularly interested in web development and the process of turning ideas into real, functional applications. This led me to focus on both frontend and backend development.

I work with technologies including HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, JavaScript, React, Node.js, Express.js, and MySQL . I enjoy creating responsive interfaces, developing application functionality, working with databases, and connecting different parts of an application into a complete system.

My development approach is centered around writing clean, organized code while creating applications that are practical, responsive, and easy to use. I believe good software should not only work correctly but should also provide a clear and enjoyable experience for its users.

I am continuously improving my skills through hands-on projects and practical development challenges. My current focus is strengthening my React and backend development abilities while building complete full-stack applications that solve real-world problems.
